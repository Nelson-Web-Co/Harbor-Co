About the Agency

Nelson's most trusted name in property.

Harbour & Co was founded on a simple principle — that buying or selling a home should be an exceptional experience, not a stressful one. For 18 years we have been Nelson's most trusted real estate agency, combining deep local knowledge with a genuinely client-first approach.

Our team of specialist agents lives and breathes the Nelson property market. Whether you're buying your first home, selling a cherished family property, or looking for your next investment, we bring the expertise, honesty and care that the moment deserves.
