Nestled in one of Atawhai's most sought-after pockets, The Orchards is a beautifully presented family home that strikes the perfect balance between contemporary style and everyday livability. Positioned just steps from the golden sands of Tahuna Beach, Bayview Retreat is an exceptional contemporary home that captures the very essence of the Nelson coastal lifestyle.
